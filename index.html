01 / THE THESISFrom expiration to opportunity

AN INDUSTRY BUILT ON TIMING

Names expire.
Possibilities emerge.

Every day, domain names move through renewal, expiration, and deletion processes. Around those transitions is an ecosystem of search, evaluation, backordering, and acquisition.

DroppyDN is a proposed identity for that ecosystem: friendly enough to remember, focused enough to own a clear position, and extensible enough to support a larger product vision.

Brand concept only. DroppyDN does not currently claim to operate a monitoring platform, marketplace, or drop-catching service.
